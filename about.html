Our Roots in Gujarat

Born from Baroda's Regal Architecture and Cultural Splendor

Vadodara is known worldwide as the cultural heart of Gujarat — a city adorned by Maharaja Sayajirao Gaekwad's visionary patronage of the fine arts, the sweeping arches of Laxmi Vilas Palace, and an innate reverence for classical design.

Moments Production was founded in 2014 to elevate regional photography above predictable trends and synthetic filters. Whether documenting high-society Gujarati nuptials, capturing heirloom portraits for royal lineages, or engineering architectural campaigns for leading developers, we apply medium-format color science and respectful documentary storytelling.

The Atelier Standards

Five Non-Negotiable Tenets

Every commission undertaken by Moments Production is governed by an exacting technical and artistic manifesto.

01

Optical Purity

We avoid synthetic software blur or AI distortions. We employ prime cine lenses, Hasselblad medium-format sensors, and Sony G-Master glass for organic depth of field and true three-dimensional subject isolation.

02

Master Color Grading

No generic downloadable filters. Every color profile is custom-graded on hardware-calibrated Eizo ColorEdge monitors to preserve the regal richness of Indian textiles and jewel tones under dynamic ceremonial lighting.

03

Archival Longevity

Photographs are meant to be held. Our albums are hand-bound with Italian leather and printed on 100% cotton rag Hahnemühle fine art papers with archival pigment inks rated to resist fading for over 150 years.

04

Quiet Discretion

During sacred rituals and high-profile private gatherings, our crew works with silent electronic shutters and unobtrusive stances. We capture intimate, tender moments without ever eclipsing the sacred flow of the ceremony.

05

Triple Redundancy

Irreplaceable moments cannot be reshot. We utilize dual-slot recording on location, immediate on-site encrypted RAID ingestion within 60 minutes of shooting, and off-site cloud vault storage for unconditional protection.
