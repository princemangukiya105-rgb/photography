The Atelier Standard

Editorial Nuance Meets Technical Rigor

Based in Alkapuri, Vadodara, Moments Production was established on the conviction that photographic memories must outlast fleeting digital trends. We reject algorithmic presets and conveyor-belt shooting in favor of authentic facial architecture, bespoke lighting geometry, and profound cultural respect.

Whether choreographing a 3-day royal Sangeet and wedding at Laxmi Vilas vicinity or sculpting a 45-minute executive headshot, our team brings Swiss Profoto lighting and Hasselblad/Sony optics to every frame.

Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding commissions, turnaround times, and studio policies.

Our flagship studio is located at Studio 402, Signature Square, Race Course Road, Alkapuri, Vadodara. We also travel for on-location, outdoor, and destination assignments across Gujarat and India.

You can connect directly with our studio via WhatsApp (+91 98765 43210), telephone, or submit our online inquiry form. Our senior production director will discuss your date, venue, custom lighting requirements, and confirm your session details directly.

Rescheduling is permitted up to 24 hours prior to studio sessions by contacting our concierge. For multi-day weddings and commercial assignments, date transfers requested at least 14 days in advance carry full credit toward a mutually available date.

Private online proofing galleries for studio portraits are delivered within 3 to 5 business days. Final master retouched high-resolution images, fine-art prints, and albums are delivered within 10 to 14 business days.
